3. Plans and fees

The Console has three plans. The pricing page shows the current prices.

  • Free costs nothing and needs no credit card. It covers up to $200 a month of managed AWS spend: the AWS costs of the resources in Stacktape-managed stacks, across all AWS accounts connected to your organization. The first time your organization goes over the limit, deploys keep working for 7 days. After that, new deploys and stacktape dev sessions pause until you upgrade or the next calendar month starts. In later months, they pause as soon as you pass the limit. Your running infrastructure is never touched.
  • Flexible has no spend limit. Its fee is a percentage of the month's managed AWS spend: 25%, falling to 19% as the spend grows.

When you upgrade to Flexible, you agree to be charged automatically every month until you downgrade. After each calendar month ends, we charge the Flexible fee for that month's managed AWS spend. Payments are processed by Paddle.
