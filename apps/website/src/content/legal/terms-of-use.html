3. Plans and fees

The Console has three plans. The pricing page shows the current prices.

  • Free costs nothing and needs no credit card. It covers up to $200 a month of managed AWS spend: the AWS costs of the resources in Stacktape-managed stacks, across all AWS accounts connected to your organization. The first time your organization's reported spend goes over the limit, you get up to seven days of grace in that UTC billing month. Once the grace ends, new deploys and stacktape dev sessions pause until you upgrade or the next month starts. In later months, the pause starts as soon as reported spend passes the limit. Work already in progress can finish and deployed resources keep running.
  • Flexible has no spend limit. Standard pricing applies one rate to the whole month's managed AWS spend, from 25% down to 19% as spend grows. We can agree to custom pricing.

When you upgrade to Flexible, you agree to automatic monthly charges through Paddle, with tax where applicable. On the 7th of each month, we check which organizations are on Flexible and use their reported managed AWS spend for the previous calendar month. This check uses the full month's reports, including spend before an upgrade.
