# Console overview

The Stacktape Console is the web app for the stacks you deploy with Stacktape. It shows your projects and stages with their deployments, logs, metrics, errors, incidents and AWS costs. It also manages team access, Git deployments, alert channels, budgets, secrets and AWS account connections.

The Console is optional. The [CLI](/cli) is open source and deploys without it; see [Use the CLI without an account](/getting-started/open-source-cli) for what needs the Console and why. The Console is free while your organization's managed AWS spend is up to $200 a month. See [Billing and subscription](/stacktape-console/billing-and-subscription).

## Get your stacks into the Console

- **You already deploy with the CLI.** Run [`stacktape connect`](/cli/connect). It signs you in and connects the AWS account of your current AWS credentials. The stacks already in that account appear as projects and stages, without a redeploy.
- **You start in the Console.** Sign in, open **AWS accounts** in the Configuration group and click **Connect AWS account**. [Connecting your AWS account](/stacktape-console/connecting-your-aws-account) walks through it. Then deploy with [`stacktape deploy`](/cli/deploy), or connect a repository for [Git deployments](/ci-cd-and-gitops/gitops-with-console).

## Find your stack and its logs

1. Open **Projects** at the top of the sidebar and select your project. A project is usually one repository, with stages such as `production` and `dev`. [Organizations, projects and stages](/stacktape-console/organizations-projects-and-stages) explains how they relate.
2. Select the stage in the row of stages in the project header. Its page has the tabs **Overview**, **Activity**, **Costs**, **Configuration**, **Diagram** and **Danger zone**. Overview lists the stage's endpoints and resources.
3. Click a resource. Its details show **Logs**, **Logs live tail**, **Metrics**, its **Configuration** and the **AWS resources** behind it. Containers also offer a **Remote session**.

The **Logs** page in the Observability group searches the logs of the whole organization. From the terminal, [`stacktape logs`](/cli/logs) reads the same logs. See [Logs](/observability/logs).

## The Projects home

**Projects** opens after you sign in. Before your first deploy, it shows how to make one and a search for stacks that are already in your connected AWS accounts. After that, **Your app** shows the stage you deployed most recently with its URL, its state and its recent deploys. The home also lists recent deployments of other projects, **Costs this month** with any budget that is over its limit, and the deploys of the last 30 days.

## What you can do in the Console

- Review deployments and AWS costs across projects and stages
- Respond to [incidents](/observability/incidents), which group signals such as a failing uptime check, a firing alarm or a new error in a production stage
- Read [logs](/observability/logs), [metrics](/observability/metrics) and [traces](/observability/tracing), and see the state of [uptime checks](/observability/uptime-checks) and [synthetic tests](/observability/synthetic-tests)
- Triage runtime errors on the [Error tracking](/observability/error-tracking) page
- Review [security findings and vulnerable packages](/stacktape-console/security-scanning) from each deployment, and set [guardrails](/guardrails/overview) that every deployment must pass
- Create [alert channels](/observability/alert-channels), [notification rules](/observability/notifications) and [budgets](/managing-costs/budgets), and check delivery in [Sent alerts](/observability/alert-history)
- Manage secrets in AWS Secrets Manager and values in AWS Systems Manager Parameter Store
- Connect [AWS accounts](/stacktape-console/connecting-your-aws-account), [Git repositories](/ci-cd-and-gitops/gitops-with-console) and [custom domains](/resources/networking/custom-domains)
- Manage [members and roles](/stacktape-console/team-and-access-control), sign in through your identity provider with [single sign-on](/stacktape-console/single-sign-on), and review the [audit log](/stacktape-console/audit-log)

Alarms, uptime checks and synthetic tests are defined in the Stacktape configuration and go live when the stack is deployed. The Console shows their state.

## Console navigation

The sidebar starts with **Projects** and **Incidents**, followed by five groups. Some items appear only when your role has the required permission; see [Permission-gated navigation](#permission-gated-navigation). Incidents, Error tracking, Security Overview and Alarms show a count when there is something to look at.

- **Organization**: Activity (the CLI operations of every project), [Costs](/managing-costs/dashboards), [Members](/stacktape-console/team-and-access-control), [API keys](/stacktape-console/api-keys), [Audit log](/stacktape-console/audit-log), [Single sign-on](/stacktape-console/single-sign-on) and [Billing](/stacktape-console/billing-and-subscription).
- **Observability**: [Logs](/observability/logs), [Metrics](/observability/metrics), [Traces](/observability/tracing), [Uptime](/observability/uptime-checks), [Synthetics](/observability/synthetic-tests) and [Error tracking](/observability/error-tracking).
- **Security**: [Overview](/stacktape-console/security-scanning) (findings and scores), Exposure (what the internet can reach), [Guardrails](/guardrails/overview) and Settings.
- **Alerting**: [Channels](/observability/alert-channels) with their [notification rules](/observability/notifications), [Sent alerts](/observability/alert-history), [Alarms](/observability/alarms) and [Budgets](/managing-costs/budgets).
- **Configuration**: [Secrets](/configuration/secrets), SSM parameters, [AWS accounts](/stacktape-console/connecting-your-aws-account), [Git connections](/ci-cd-and-gitops/gitops-with-console), [Runners](/ci-cd-and-gitops/build-runners), Third-party providers and [Domains](/resources/networking/custom-domains).

The account menu in the header has **Account** (your [account settings](/stacktape-console/account-settings)) and **Sign out**. **Subscription plans** opens from Billing.

## Common tasks

### Viewing deployment activity

Open **Activity** in the Organization group. The page lists Stacktape CLI operations across your organization, so you can see who ran deploys, deletes, scripts, bucket syncs and other recorded commands. The **Activity** tab of a stage lists the operations of that stage.

For each recorded operation, the CLI sends the command, the project, stage, region and AWS account, the Git branch, commit and repository URL, the Stacktape version, timing and the result. It also sends the flags that name the target and change how the command runs, such as `--stage`, `--region`, `--config`, `--full`, `--auto-rollback`, `--no-cache`, `--target-version`, `--script-name`, `--resource-name` and `--bucket-id`. Flags whose value is free-form input are never sent: `--docker-args`, script `--env` values, commands, SDK inputs, secret values and API keys stay on your machine or CI runner.

### Managing secrets

Open **Secrets** in the Configuration group to manage sensitive values stored in AWS Secrets Manager. Secrets created here can be referenced in your Stacktape configuration. The page requires the `secrets:view` permission. See [Secrets](/configuration/secrets).

### Managing SSM parameters

Open **SSM parameters** in the Configuration group to manage values stored in AWS Systems Manager Parameter Store. Use parameters for non-sensitive configuration your workloads need at runtime, such as feature flags or endpoint URLs. Click **New parameter** to add one. The page requires the `ssm-params:view` permission.

### Setting up alarms

Alarms are defined in the Stacktape configuration, in the `alarms` list of a resource, and are created when the stack is deployed. The **Alarms** page in the Alerting group shows the current state of the CloudWatch alarms in your connected AWS accounts. See [Alarms](/observability/alarms).

Alarm rules created in the Console earlier are legacy. They are listed under **Legacy alarm rules** on the Alarms page and keep applying to matching deployments until the stack's configuration defines its alarms. Run `stacktape alarm export` to move them into your configuration.

### Tracking runtime errors

The **Error tracking** page in the Observability group is an error inbox for your deployed workloads. Stacktape reads runtime errors from your logs and groups repeated occurrences into one entry per distinct error and resource. Error tracking is on for every stage by default; a stack turns it off or limits it to some stages with `deploymentConfig.errorTracking`. The page requires the `issues:view` permission. See [Error tracking](/observability/error-tracking).

### Inviting members

Open **Members** in the Organization group and click **Invite members**. Each member has a role that determines what they can view and change. The page requires the `members:view` permission. See [Team and access control](/stacktape-console/team-and-access-control).

### Setting budgets

Open **Budgets** in the Alerting group and click **New budget**. Each budget monitors one connected AWS account for the calendar month. Stacktape creates the AWS budget when you save it and removes it when you delete the budget. The **Alerts sent** section on the same page lists the budget alerts that were sent. See [Budgets](/managing-costs/budgets).

### Configuring alert channels and notification rules

Channels are the destinations where alerts are delivered, such as a Slack channel, an email address or a webhook. Open **Channels** in the Alerting group and click **New channel**. The new channel's page opens; click **New rule** there to choose which events (for example a failed deployment, an opened incident or a changed secret) it receives. Adding rules requires the `org:manage-notifications` permission. **Sent alerts** lists every alert that was sent and its delivery status per channel.

## Permission-gated navigation

The Console uses role-based access control. Sidebar items are hidden when your role lacks the required permission:

| Navigation item | Required permission |
|---|---|
| Incidents | `incidents:view` |
| Members | `members:view` |
| API keys | `api-keys:manage-own` (not Viewers) |
| Audit log | `audit-log:view` (Owners and Admins) |
| Single sign-on | `sso:manage` (Owners only) |
| Costs, Billing | `org:view-billing` (Owners and Admins) |
| Error tracking | `issues:view` |
| Security group | `security:view` |
| Alarms | `org:manage-alarms` |
| Budgets | `org:manage-budgets` |
| Secrets | `secrets:view` |
| SSM parameters, Third-party providers | `ssm-params:view` |
| Git connections | Not Viewers |
| Domains | `domains:manage` |

Projects, Activity, Logs, Metrics, Traces, Uptime, Synthetics, Channels, Sent alerts, AWS accounts and Runners are shown to every role. Every role that sees the Security group can read Guardrails; only owners and admins (`org:manage-guardrails`) can change them.

## Troubleshooting

### Cost data is not appearing

Cost reports are generated daily from AWS Cost and Usage Reports and may take up to a day to appear. If the Costs page shows no data, check that you have a connected [AWS account](/stacktape-console/connecting-your-aws-account) and allow up to 24 hours for data to arrive.

### An alarm does not exist in AWS

Alarms are created when the stack is deployed. After you add an alarm to a resource's `alarms` list, run [`stacktape deploy`](/cli/deploy) for the target stage. Legacy Console alarm rules also take effect only on the next deployment of each matching stage.

### The Error tracking page is empty despite errors in logs

A stack reports errors only after it has been deployed with Stacktape v4 while signed in to the Console, because the log subscriptions are added during that deployment. Stacks you deployed without an account and brought in with `stacktape connect` need one signed-in deploy. Click **Settings** on the Error tracking page to open **Error tracking settings**: it shows whether error tracking is on for the organization and lists each deployed stack with whether its last deployment wired error tracking. Also check that the stack's `deploymentConfig.errorTracking` does not turn it off for that stage, and that the resource does not use `logging.logClass: infrequent-access`. See [Error tracking](/observability/error-tracking).

### Sidebar items are missing

Some sidebar items need a permission your role may not have, for example Costs or Alarms. The [permission table](#permission-gated-navigation) lists them. An Owner or Admin of your organization can change your role; see [Team and access control](/stacktape-console/team-and-access-control#roles).

## FAQ

### Does Stacktape deploy into my own AWS account?

Yes. Stacktape uses a cross-account IAM role to manage resources in your AWS account, so you never hand over long-lived AWS credentials. Connect more accounts from **AWS accounts** in the Configuration group. See [Connecting your AWS account](/stacktape-console/connecting-your-aws-account) for the required role.
